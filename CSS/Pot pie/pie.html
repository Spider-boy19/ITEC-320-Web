George's Chicken Pot Pie Recipe

one 12 inch pie

10 Min - Prep Time

40 Min - Cook Time

50 Min - Total

6 Servings

INGREDIENTS

  • 1 TBSP Olive Oil
  • 1.5 lbs bonesless skinless chicken breast, cubed
  • Salt and pepper to Tase
  • 1/2 White Onion Chopped
  • 2 Garlic Cloves, Minced
  • 1 Yukon Gold Potatos, cubed
  • 2 Cups Frozen Peas and Carrots
  • 4 Tbsp all-purpose butter
  • 4 Tbsp Butter
  • 2 Cups Chicken Broth
  • 1 Frozen prepared pie dough, thawed
  • 1 Egg, Beaten

Directions

  1. Prehead Over: Preheat oven to 400degrees F(200 degrees C)
  2. Cook the Chicken
    • Heat the Oil in a cast-Iron Pan
    • Add the chicken, season with salt and pepper
    • Cook the meat until Godlen brown, about 6 min
    • Remove Chicken and set aside
  3. Prepare the veggie Mixture
    • Add onions and garlic to the skillet, saute ultil translucent
    • Add Potato cubes, saute around 5 minutes
    • Add peas the carrots, stir
    • Add butter, allow it to melt
    • Sprinle flour on top. Cover Veggies, stir to avoid bumps
    • Add Chicken Broth bring to a boil to thicken
    • Season with salt and peper, remove from heat
  4. Bake The pie
    • Add the cicken back into the mixture. stir to distrubute meat
    • Place Pie dough over the mixture, seal along the endges of skillet with fingers or fork
    • Take the beaten Egg and brush the top of the pastry
    • Cut 3 slits in the pastry, this is to release steam during cooking
    • Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, or until golden brown
